# Reel background photos

Put your own photos here (JPG or PNG, any size; portrait is best). Good subjects: platforms, ticket gates,
shinkansen, station signs, airport trains. Use your own photos only, or photos you have the right to use.
Name them by subject, for example `shinkansen-platform.jpg` or `ticket-gate.jpg`.

The daily reel run picks one that fits the article and sets "photo" in spec.json. Without a photo,
gen.py uses the animated station-style background.
