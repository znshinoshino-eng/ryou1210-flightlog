## Shopping
- 2025: shopping ¥61,136 per visitor, 27.0% of spending (34.7% in 2019); lodging is now the largest share. https://www.mlit.go.jp/kankocho/content/002020046.pdf
- US visitors 2024 (JTA via JTB Foundation): other food 52.7%, clothing 52.6%, sweets 51.7%, alcohol 29.7%, crafts 20.6%, cosmetics only 17.9%. https://www.jtb.or.jp/book/wp-content/uploads/sites/4/2025/10/nenpo2025_2_1.pdf
- Tax-free switched to the refund method for purchases from 1 Nov 2026 → article tax-free-refund-japan-airport.

## Transport
- Rail used by ~80% of visitors; shinkansen 23.4%; JR Pass ~5.7% (secondary analysis of JTA 2024 data, https://crirc.jp/about/pdf/250827_nakashima.pdf).
- JTA visitor-friendliness survey FY2025 (n=4,110): top problems — trash bins 17.2%, communication 15.4%, crowding 12.9%, using transport 11.3%, signs 10.9%, card payments 10.7%. Within transport trouble: finding routes 45%, finding platform/stop 38%, buying tickets 26%; non-shinkansen rail 62%, buses 30%. https://www.mlit.go.jp/kankocho/content/001998583.pdf
- English-speaking visitors: 12–15 nights, spend ~2x average on transport (JTA 2025).
- JR Pass from 1 Oct 2026: ¥53,000 (7-day) via overseas agencies; official site keeps ¥50,000 "for a limited time" (JR Central https://global.jr-central.co.jp/en/news/_pdf/2026/0001.pdf) → price text fixed in EN + 5 translations.
