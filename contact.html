Contact

Thanks for visiting Flightlog Japan. If you have a question about one of our guides, spotted something that's out of date, or want to get in touch about a partnership, please send us a message using the form below.

Open the contact form →

Before you write

  • We read every message, but replies can take a few days.
  • We can't make bookings, issue tickets or handle refunds for third-party services. For those, please contact the service provider directly.
  • For urgent problems while traveling (lost items, delays, emergencies), contact the station staff, the operator, or local emergency services.

Corrections

Fares, timetables and rules in Japan change often. If you find information that's wrong or outdated, please tell us which page and what changed. We'll check and update it.

Your information

We use the details you send only to reply to your message. See our privacy policy for more.
