Snow Day Trips from Tokyo by Shinkansen: GALA Yuzawa and Echigo-Yuzawa

Day-trip guide · Written by someone who works on Japan's railways · Checked November 2026

Short answer: the easiest snow day from Tokyo is GALA Yuzawa, a ski resort with its own seasonal shinkansen station. JR East says it is from 71 minutes from Tokyo Station, and when you walk out of the ticket gate you are already in the resort's base building. For the 2026-27 season, the slopes are scheduled to open on 19 December 2026. If GALA is busy or you want a different hill, change at Echigo-Yuzawa, one stop earlier, where several other resorts are a short walk or bus ride away.

This guide covers the season dates, which trains to take, lift tickets, how to carry skis or a snowboard on JR East trains, and a simple plan for the day.

GALA Yuzawa: the ski resort with its own station

GALA Yuzawa Station is one stop beyond Echigo-Yuzawa on the Joetsu Shinkansen. JR East's timetable lists it as "GALA Yuzawa(Seasonal)", and its JR Tokyo Wide Pass page notes that "Gala Yuzawa Station only operates during the winter and spring." So outside the season, you can't take the shinkansen all the way there.

The station is built into Cowabunga, the resort's base ski centre. GALA's English site says the main ticket counters and information desk are on the 2nd floor, and rental is in the same building. The gondola then takes you up to Cheers, the centre at the top of the gondola.

For 2026-27, GALA's official season schedule is:

PeriodWhat's open
12 – 18 December 2026Gondola and sightseeing lifts only (no skiing)
19 December 2026 – 5 May 2027Slopes open

One change this season: GALA has suspended its South Area for 2026-27, including the ropeway link to neighbouring Yuzawa Kogen. GALA says the connection to Yuzawa Kogen will be by shuttle bus instead. This season GALA is operating the Central and North areas.

Which shinkansen to take

GALA Yuzawa is served by the Tanigawa trains on the Joetsu Shinkansen. In its October 2026 press release on winter extra trains, JR East says it will run shinkansen to GALA Yuzawa every day, and lists these morning and evening services for skiers:

These are only some of the trains. JR East also extends some regular Tanigawa services to GALA Yuzawa during the season, so check the timetable and running dates for your exact date on the JR East timetable site or when you book.

Tickets: single tickets or the JR Tokyo Wide Pass

You can buy shinkansen tickets from Tokyo to GALA Yuzawa (or to Echigo-Yuzawa) at ticket machines and counters, or online on JR East's Eki-net (JR-EAST Train Reservation). Fares depend on the train and seat type, so check the price for your trip there. Reserving a seat in advance is a good idea.

If you're doing more than a day trip, look at the JR TOKYO Wide Pass. At the time of writing (November 2026), JR East sells it for ¥16,000 for adults (12 and older) and ¥8,000 for children (6 to 11), valid for three consecutive days from the day you choose. JR East's own example route for the pass is Tokyo Station to GALA Yuzawa on the Joetsu Shinkansen. Green Car and GranClass seats cost extra, and the pass can't be used on the Tokaido Shinkansen. Compare it with two single tickets plus anything else you plan to do in those three days. Our rail pass guide explains how to do the math.

GALA also lists a cheaper "Gondola & Balloche" ticket (¥6,500 adult, ¥5,500 online). For preschool children, GALA says up to two ride free with each adult, and a third child pays the child price.

If you're planning ahead, GALA is selling an early-bird lift ticket for ¥4,800 (instead of the regular ¥8,000) until 30 November 2026 at 23:00. Check GALA's site for the conditions before you buy.

Rental: skis, snowboards, boots and ski wear can be rented in Cowabunga, next to the station. GALA's rental page says online reservations are accepted until 23:00 two days before your visit, paid by credit card. If you're coming in a busy period, booking ahead saves queueing on the day.

Carrying your own skis or snowboard on the train

If you bring your own gear, JR East's oversized luggage guide has the rules for its trains:

  • Size: up to 250 cm for length + width + height, and up to 2 m long.
  • Weight: 30 kg or less per item.
  • Number: up to 2 items (not counting things like umbrellas and handbags). Free of charge.
  • Long sports gear: JR East says sports equipment and similar items may be over the length limit if they can be stored upright on board.
  • No reservation needed on the Joetsu Shinkansen. Storage space is first come, first served, and JR East doesn't take reservations for luggage space alone. Some trains have extra storage areas for skis and snowboards.

JR East also says items over the limits "may not be brought on board", and asks you to refrain from putting heavy or irregularly shaped luggage on the overhead racks. Keep bags out of the aisles and doorways. Note that JR East refers you to the operators' own websites for the Tokaido, Sanyo and Kyushu Shinkansen, which have their own oversized-luggage policies. Our shinkansen guide covers that.

For a day trip, the simplest option is to travel light and rent everything at the resort. If you're on a longer trip with a big suitcase, consider sending it ahead or leaving it in a station locker.

Echigo-Yuzawa: more resorts, one stop earlier

Echigo-Yuzawa is the stop before GALA Yuzawa, and many other ski areas are close to the station. The Yuzawa tourism organization lists these access times from the station:

JR East also mentions a free shuttle bus from Echigo-Yuzawa Station to GALA Yuzawa, taking about 10 minutes and operating only on the resort's business days. That's useful if your train doesn't continue to GALA. Check the latest shuttle timetable with the resort before you go.

A simple day plan

  1. Morning: An early Tanigawa from Tokyo Station (Tanigawa 71 or 73, leaving at 6:24 or 6:44, if one runs on your date). Book a reserved seat on weekends.
  2. On arrival: Go up to the 2nd floor of Cowabunga for lift tickets, pick up your rental, and take the gondola up to Cheers.
  3. Afternoon: Ski or snowboard, or just enjoy the snow.
  4. Evening: Return your rental and catch a Tanigawa back to Tokyo, for example the 17:04 from GALA Yuzawa, arriving at 18:20.

Make sure your phone has data for checking timetables on the go (see our eSIM and Wi-Fi guide), and for more easy trips out of Tokyo, try onsen towns by train or a day in Nikko.
