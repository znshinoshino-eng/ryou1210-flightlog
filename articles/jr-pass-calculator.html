JR Pass Calculator: Is the Japan Rail Pass Worth It for Your Trip?

Free tool · Made by someone who works on Japan's railways · Prices checked October 2026

Short answer: add the long-distance trips you plan to take, pick a pass, and the calculator compares the pass price with buying each shinkansen ticket on its own. Every price comes from JR's own official pages.

JR Pass calculator

    No trips added yet. Tip: add each direction separately, for example Tokyo → Kyoto and Kyoto → Tokyo.

    How the calculator works

    • Pass prices are the adult ordinary-car prices from the official JAPAN RAIL PASS website: ¥50,000 (7 days), ¥80,000 (14 days) and ¥100,000 (21 days) when bought on the official site, or ¥53,000, ¥84,000 and ¥105,000 through a JR-designated travel agency outside Japan.
    • Single-ticket prices are JR Central's official smartEX prices for a reserved ordinary seat on the Nozomi (or Mizuho), one way, adult, regular season. Buying the same ticket at a station counter costs a little more, so the calculator is slightly generous to single tickets.
    • Seasons: JR adds or takes off a small amount in peak and off-peak seasons (¥200 to ¥800 depending on the route and season). It rarely changes the answer.
    • Children (6–11): the pass is exactly half price, and fares are roughly half too, so the result is about the same.
    • Routes not in the list (for example Tokyo → Kanazawa, Sendai or Nagano on JR East): look up the fare and add it with "Another JR ride".

    One catch: the Nozomi

    The JR Pass doesn't cover the fastest Nozomi and Mizuho trains on its own. Pass holders usually take the Hikari, which is a little slower, or buy JR Central's special Nozomi ticket for pass holders. At the time of writing it costs ¥4,960 for Tokyo – Kyoto or Shin-Osaka, ¥6,500 for Tokyo – Hiroshima, and ¥4,960 for Nagoya – Hiroshima. If you plan to buy it, add it with "Another JR ride".

    What to do next

    • The pass wins? Buy it before you fly, then reserve seats for free. JR Pass on Klook (affiliate link). Prices there can differ from the official website, so compare before you buy.
    • Single tickets win? Book them on smartEX, or in your own currency on Klook: Tokyo to Kyoto shinkansen (affiliate link), Tokyo to Osaka shinkansen (affiliate link). A cheaper regional pass may also save money where you spend the most time.
    • New to the bullet train? Read how to ride the shinkansen, including the 160 cm luggage rule.
