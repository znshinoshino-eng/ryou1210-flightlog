Kyoto and Osaka Sightseeing: Tickets and Tours Worth Booking Ahead

Planning guide · Written by someone who works on Japan's railways · Prices and ratings checked on Klook in October 2026

Short answer: in Kyoto, book the Sagano Romantic Train and teamLab Biovortex Kyoto before you go, because both can sell out. The Hozugawa river boat pairs perfectly with the train for a half day in Arashiyama. In Osaka, the Osaka Amazing Pass pays off if you'll visit several of its included sights in one day. Kaiyukan aquarium is easier with a pre-booked time, and the Umeda Sky Building can usually be decided on the day.

This is the Kansai version of our Tokyo sightseeing bookings guide. Ratings, booking counts and prices below are what Klook showed at the time of writing. Prices are "from" prices and change by date and option.

Kyoto: the Arashiyama combo (Sagano train + Hozugawa boat)

This is the classic Arashiyama half day. You ride the Sagano Romantic Train out through the Hozu Gorge to Kameoka, then come back down the same river by Hozugawa boat, ending in Arashiyama.

Sagano Romantic Train

A slow scenic train running from Torokko Saga Station, through Torokko Arashiyama and Torokko Hozukyo, to Torokko Kameoka. The operator describes it as about a 25-minute ride along the gorge.

  • Why book ahead: reserved seats can sell out. Online sales open at midnight one month before the travel date. Same-day tickets are sold first-come, first-served at the station counters (not at Torokko Hozukyo), and once reserved seats are gone, a limited number of standing tickets go on sale on the day.
  • The open-air car: Car No. 5, "The Rich", has no window glass. You choose it when you buy. Umbrellas aren't allowed on board, so bring a raincoat if rain is forecast.
  • Season: it doesn't run in winter. In 2026 the winter break is 1 January to 28 February and 30–31 December, and some days in season have no service, so check the operator's calendar.
  • On Klook: rated 4.5 from 4.8K reviews, 200K+ booked, from US$5.59 at the time of writing.

Hozugawa river boat

A boat ride down about 16 km of the Hozu River, from Kameoka to Arashiyama. It takes about two hours.

  • When it runs: most of the year. In 2026, from 10 March to 13 December, boats leave every hour from 9:00 to 15:00 on weekdays, and as they fill on weekends and holidays. From 5 January to 9 March 2026 there were four sailings a day (10:00, 11:30, 13:00, 14:30). It's closed from 29 December to 4 January and on a few safety-check days.
  • Getting there from the train: from Torokko Kameoka Station, a local bus takes about 10 minutes to the boarding point. Buses are not frequent, so head straight to the stop after you get off.
  • If the train is full: you can still do the boat. Get to Kameoka by JR or bus instead.
  • On Klook: rated 4.7 from 3.3K reviews, 200K+ booked, from US$37.95 at the time of writing.

See times on Hozugawa River Boat Ride on Klook (affiliate link). There's no combined discount for the train and boat, so you book them separately. For trains from Kyoto Station to Saga-Arashiyama, see our guide to getting around Kyoto and Osaka.

Kyoto: teamLab, Gion and a day tour

teamLab Biovortex Kyoto

A permanent teamLab digital art museum that opened on 7 October 2025. It's about a 7-minute walk from Kyoto Station's Hachijo East Gate, so it's easy to fit in on arrival or departure day, or on a rainy afternoon. Opening hours are 9:00 to 21:00 with last entry at 19:30 at the time of writing.

  • Why book ahead: tickets are for a set date and time, and teamLab says they may sell out. You can buy at the door if any are left, but it costs more.
  • On Klook: rated 4.8 from 3.2K reviews, 200K+ booked, from US$25.29 at the time of writing.

Pick a slot on teamLab Biovortex Kyoto tickets on Klook (affiliate link).

Gion walking tour

Gion is lovely to wander on your own, but a guide explains what you're actually looking at and keeps you on the right side of local rules. Kyoto's official tourism site asks visitors not to stop, touch, follow, or take unauthorized photos or videos of geiko, and not to block the street while taking photos. The Gion small-group walking tour on Klook is rated 4.9 from 1.3K reviews, with 10K+ booked at the time of writing.

Kyoto & Nara day tour (if you're short on time)

If you only have one day for Kyoto and Nara, a tour saves you working out the connections. The most-booked one on Klook leaves from Osaka or Kyoto, and its listing includes the Sagano train, Nara Park, Fushimi Inari and Arashiyama. It's rated 4.9 from 10.9K reviews, with 100K+ booked at the time of writing.

Osaka: Amazing Pass, Kaiyukan and Umeda Sky

Osaka Amazing Pass: who it pays off for

The pass combines a day of trains and buses with free entry to about 40 sights. At the time of writing, the official price is ¥3,500 for 1 day and ¥5,000 for 2 consecutive days. It covers Osaka Metro, Osaka City Bus (some routes excluded) and the New Tram, plus Hankyu, Hanshin, Keihan, Kintetsu and Nankai trains mainly within Osaka City. JR lines are not on its list. A "day" runs from 3:00 a.m. to 2:59 a.m., and each included sight can be entered once.

  • Good for: a full sightseeing day with several included sights, such as the Umeda Sky Building, the Osaka Castle Museum (which includes the castle's Main Tower) and the Tombori River Cruise.
  • Watch the small print: Umeda Sky Building is free with the pass only until 15:00 (after that you get 10% off). Some sights, like the river cruises and Tsutenkaku, need a time slot on the day.
  • Not worth it: on a Kaiyukan or USJ day. Neither is included. For transport only, Osaka Metro's own Enjoy Eco Card (¥820 on weekdays, ¥620 at weekends and holidays for adults) is far cheaper. See our Kyoto and Osaka transport guide.
  • On Klook: rated 4.7 from 21.7K reviews, 1M+ booked, from US$20.75 at the time of writing.

Buy it through Osaka Amazing Pass on Klook (affiliate link).

Osaka Aquarium Kaiyukan

One of the largest aquariums in the world, built around a huge Pacific Ocean tank with a whale shark. It's about a 5-minute walk from exit 1 of Osakako Station on the Osaka Metro Chuo Line.

  • Why book ahead: the aquarium itself recommends e-tickets with a set date and time. With a same-day ticket you may not get in at the time you want. Prices vary by date. Children aged 2 and under are free.
  • On Klook: rated 4.8 from 14.9K reviews, 600K+ booked. Prices depend on the ticket type.

Check dates on Osaka Aquarium Kaiyukan tickets on Klook (affiliate link).

Umeda Sky Building (Kuchu Teien Observatory)

The observatory links the building's two towers on the 39th and 40th floors and the roof, which is an open-air 360-degree deck. It's open 9:30 to 22:30 (last entry 22:00) at the time of writing, and the rooftop may close in heavy rain. It's walkable from JR Osaka Station and the Umeda stations.

  • Book ahead? Optional. If you have the Amazing Pass, go before 15:00. For an evening visit, a separate ticket is simpler.
  • On Klook: rated 4.7 from 10.0K reviews, 600K+ booked.

Get a ticket through Umeda Sky Building tickets on Klook (affiliate link). Going to Universal Studios Japan as well? See our USJ tickets guide.

A simple way to plan your days

  • A month out: book the Sagano Romantic Train as soon as your date opens, then the Hozugawa boat to match. Book teamLab Kyoto and any tour you want.
  • Arashiyama day: morning train from Torokko Saga to Kameoka, bus to the boat, about two hours on the river, then lunch and the rest of the afternoon in Arashiyama.
  • Osaka pass day: start early on the Amazing Pass, do Umeda Sky before 15:00, then Osaka Castle and a river cruise.
  • Kaiyukan day: book a time slot and use an IC card or the Enjoy Eco Card instead of the Amazing Pass.
  • Rainy day: teamLab Kyoto or Kaiyukan.
