About Flightlog Japan

Who this is for

Flightlog Japan is written for visitors to Japan who are into aviation — plane spotters, avgeeks, or anyone who just wants a good photo of a jumbo jet against a Tokyo sunset. The goal is simple: tell you exactly where to go, how to get there, and when, without the vague "visit a famous spot" advice you get from generic travel sites.

Who's behind it

This site is run by a Tokyo-based photographer who shoots at Haneda regularly (Sony α7 IV with a SIGMA telephoto lens) and also has hands-on, detailed knowledge of how Japan's train and railway system works — so the access and ticketing advice here comes from real, practical experience, not guesswork. More photography content (in Japanese) is published on note.com under the name "ryou1210."
